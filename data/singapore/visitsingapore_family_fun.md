[![Image 17: Thrilling water slides at 'Adventure Cove'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/adventure-cove-d.jpg) #### Adventure Cove Have a splashing good time at Adventure Cove, experience thrilling water slides, snorkel with fishes and relax on the lazy river.](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/adventure-cove-waterpark/)

[![Image 18: Curiosity Cove](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/746x620-mandai-curiousity_cove.jpg) #### Curiosity Cove Discover Singapore’s largest indoor nature-inspired playscape for kids 12 and under. Through interactive and educational play, children explore how animals live, adapt and thrive in the wild across four immersive zones filled with wonder and discovery.](https://www.mandai.com/en/curiosity-cove.html?cmp=sgcuriosity_cove%7Cref%7Cvs%7Cpar%7Cstb%7Cthings_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_family_fun)

[![Image 19: Science Centre](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/science-center.jpg) #### Science Centre Explore a world of discovery at Science Centre Singapore, where hands-on exhibits, imaginative play at KidsSTOP™, and immersive 8K 3D dome experiences at the Omni-Theatre inspire curious minds of all ages to discover, learn, and imagine together.](https://www.science.edu.sg/)

[![Image 20: Singapore Oceanarium](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/sea-aquarium-d.jpg) #### Singapore Oceanarium Dive into a mesmerizing underwater world with over 40,000 marine animals across 120 species. Encounter deep-sea creatures, sea jellies and witness the magic of the ocean.](https://www.rwsentosa.com/en/play/singapore-oceanarium)

[![Image 21: Wild Wild Wet Water Park Adventure](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/wild-wild-wet-d.jpg) #### Wild Wild Wet Slide, splash and have an exciting water park adventure with endless excitement for the whole family.](https://www.wildwildwet.com/)

[![Image 22: Mandai Bird Paradise - Water Foundation](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/birds-paradise-d.jpg) #### Bird Paradise Take flight to Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species. Explore 10 immersive walk-through aviaries, inspired by habitats from around the world.](https://www.mandai.com/en/bird-paradise.html?cmp=sg%7Cbpd%7Cref%7Cvs%7Cpar%7Cstb%7C%7C%7Cthings_to_do_family_fun&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_family_fun)

[![Image 23: Disney Cruise Line](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/b28fb4d1-74cd-41ca-b041-cd1b92450d94.jpeg) #### Disney Cruise Line Adventure is calling! With 7 uniquely themed areas brimming with fun and relaxation, world-class entertainment and signature Disney dining, the all-new Disney Adventure will set sail from Singapore. Bookings now open.](https://disneycruise.disney.go.com/en-sg/ships/adventure/?CMP=NLC-DCLFY25Q1DCLEDM0006)

[![Image 24: Sentosa island of Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/sentosa-island-d.jpg) #### Sentosa Island Beach play, thrilling attractions and family time await you at Sentosa Island.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/sentosa-island/)

[![Image 25: Singapore Zoo, an award winning wildlife park](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/780x780-mandai_singapore_zoo.jpg) #### Singapore Zoo Visit Singapore's only rainforest zoo, renowned for its naturalist, cageless habitats. From the furry and adorable to the ferocious and majestic, the animals of the Singapore Zoo are a sight to behold.](https://www.mandai.com/en/singapore-zoo.html?cmp=sg|sz|ref|vs|par|stb|||things_to_do_family_fun&ut%20m_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content-things_to_do_family_fun)

[![Image 26: Trampoline fun at Bounce Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/bounce-singapore-d.jpg) #### Bounce Singapore Bounce, flip and play on trampolines, bringing active fun to the whole family.](https://bounceinc.com.sg/)

[![Image 27: Discover river wildlife at 'River Wonders'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/780x440-family_fun-river_wonder.jpg) #### River Wonders Immerse yourself in Asia's first and only river-themed wildlife park, home to one of the world's largest collections of freshwater vertebrates. Explore iconic rivers and encounter resident giants like the pandas, Mekong catfish and more.](https://www.mandai.com/en/river-wonders.html?cmp=sg|rw|ref|vs|par|stb||things_to_do_family_fun&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_family_fun)

[![Image 28: Multi-sensory learning experience at Singapore Discovery Centre](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/singapore-discovery-centre-d.jpg) #### Singapore Discovery Centre Unleash your curiosity, explore interactive exhibits and discover the wonders of a multi-sensory learning experience as a family.](https://www.defencecollectivesg.com/singapore-discovery-centre)

[![Image 29: World famous Universal Studio in Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/family-fun/universal-studios-singapore-d.jpg) #### Universal Studios Singapore Experience the magic and thrills at Universal Studios Singapore, where blockbuster movies come to life in a world of endless entertainment.](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/universal-studios-singapore/)
