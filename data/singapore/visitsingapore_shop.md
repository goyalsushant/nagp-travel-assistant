[![Image 24: TANGS Orchard, a beloved department store in SIngapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/shop/tangs.jpg) #### TANGS Orchard A beloved department store in Singapore, TANGS offers a curated selection of fashion, beauty and homeware.](https://tangs.com/)

[![Image 25: Funan, a tech paradise](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/shop/funan.jpg) #### Funan A tech paradise where innovation meets lifestyle, Funan offers cutting-edge gadgets and hip fashion finds.](https://www.capitaland.com/sg/malls/funan/en.html)

[![Image 26: Discover colourful Katong Joo Chiat heritage shophouses](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/katong-joo-chiat-d.jpg) #### Katong-Joo Chiat Discover the enchanting blend of Peranakan heritage, charming streets, and boutiques in the Katong-Joo Chiat neighborhood.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/katong-joo-chiat/)

[![Image 27: Mustafa Centre, one stop destination for shopping](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/shop/mustafa-centre.jpg) #### Mustafa Centre Shop around the clock for almost anything, from electronics, to textiles and jewelry.](https://www.instagram.com/mustafacentre/?hl=en)
