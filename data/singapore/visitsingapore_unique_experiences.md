[![Image 17: AltitudeX at Sentosa Island](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/ifly-d.jpg) #### AltitudeX Defy gravity, soar indoors and feel the adrenaline of flight at AltitudeX on Sentosa Island.](https://altitudex.com/sg/)

[![Image 18: Engage in nature's healing process at Forest with You](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/forest-bathing-by-xiu-nature-connections-d.jpg) #### Forest Bathing by Forest with You Embrace nature's healing touch, immerse in lush greenery and rejuvenate amidst the serenity of a forest.](https://www.forestwithyou.com/)

[![Image 19: scaling the rock faces of Rainforest Wild Adventure](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/1064x600-tour-rainforest_wild.jpg) #### Rainforest Wild Adventure Glide through the canopy or swing high into the sky with Adventure+ activities, taking in heart-racing views while spotting animals beneath. Don't miss the exclusive Okapi and Diana Monkey, now in Singapore for the first time, and many more!](https://www.mandai.com/en/rainforest-wild-adventure.html?%20cmp=sg|rfw_adventure|ref|vs|par|stb|||things_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_unique_experiences)

[![Image 20: Ubin Bisect Group Kayaking](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/ubin-bisect-kayaking-d.jpg) #### Ubin Bisect Kayaking Glide through serene mangroves and encounter diverse wildlife, connecting with nature at Pulau Ubin.](https://www.adventures.network/ubin-bisect-kayaking)

[![Image 21: Mandai Bird Paradise - Water Foundation](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/birds-paradise-d.jpg) #### Bird Paradise Take flight to Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species. Explore 10 immersive walk-through aviaries, inspired by habitats from around the world.](https://www.mandai.com/en/bird-paradise.html?cmp=sg%7Cbpd%7Cref%7Cvs%7Cpar%7Cstb%7C%7C%7Cthings_to_do_family_fun&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_family_fun)

[![Image 22: Kulnari Mystery Golf - Play Mini Golf](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/singapore-river/title-slider/746x746-singapore_river-kulnari.jpg) #### Kulnari Mystery Golf Solve intriguing puzzles, play mini golf and unravel thrilling mysteries in this immersive mystery golf course.](https://www.kulnari.com/)

[![Image 23: Vibrant surroundings at Synthesis Bar Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/synthesis-bar-d.jpg) #### Synthesis Bar Savour mixology magic, tasting eclectic concoctions amidst chic and vibrant surroundings.](https://www.synthesis.sg/)

[![Image 24: Exploria](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/1064x600-tour-exploria.jpg) #### Exploria Explore one of Southeast Asia's largest indoor, nature-themed multimedia attractions, where rarely seen realms of the world come to life. Use your RFID wristband to unlock interactive touchpoints and discover the wonders of nature — past and present.](https://www.mandai.com/en/exploria.html?%20cmp=sg|exploria|ref|vs|par|stb|||things_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_unique_experiences)

[![Image 25: Naturalist Night Adventure at Sentosa](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/naturalist-night-adventure-d.jpg) #### Naturalist Night Adventure Embark on a thrilling wildlife safari adventure, spotting nocturnal creatures under the moonlit sky at Sentosa Island.](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/events/sentosa-naturalist-night-adventure/)

[![Image 26: indoor skate and surfing experience at Trifecta](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/unique-experiences/trifecta-d.jpg) #### Trifecta Experience the ultimate thrill and unleash your adventurous spirit at Trifecta. Hit the snowboard, ride the waves, and feel the excitement of the skate bowl.](https://trifectasingapore.com/)
