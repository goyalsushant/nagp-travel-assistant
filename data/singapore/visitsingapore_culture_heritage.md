# Top Things To Do - Culture & Heritage

Walk through Singapore’s neighbourhoods to glimpse its past and present. Take a look at how Singapore preserves and promotes its cultural heritage.

## Culture & Heritage

From national monuments to community projects, discover why Singapore was named Best Cultural Destination in the World (Tripadvisor's 2026 Travellers' Choice Awards).   
  
For upcoming culture and heritage tours, talks and events, you may wish to check out [Catch.sg](https://www.catch.sg/Event?Genre=Culture+Heritage&utm_source=visitsingapore&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vs_culture_heritage&utm_term=), Singapore's national platform on all things arts and culture.

[![Image 17: Explore Chinatown to embrace Chinese heritage](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/chinatown-d.jpg) #### Chinatown Embrace Chinese heritage through bustling streets, vibrant culture and delectable cuisine in Chinatown.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/)

[![Image 18: Discover colourful Katong Joo Chiat heritage shophouses](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/katong-joo-chiat-d.jpg) #### Katong-Joo Chiat Discover the enchanting blend of Peranakan heritage, charming streets, and boutiques in the Katong-Joo Chiat neighborhood.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/katong-joo-chiat/)

[![Image 19: Traditional art & artifacts at Peranakan Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/peranakan-museum-d.jpg) #### Peranakan Musuem Delve into the vibrant Peranakan culture and explore the traditional art and artifacts at the Peranakan Museum.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/peranakan-museum/)

[![Image 20: Hajjah Fatimah Mosque Majestic Architecture](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/hajjah-fatimah-mosque-d.jpg) #### Hajjah Fatimah Mosque Experience a mix of Islamic and European architectural styles, rich in heritage and spirituality.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/kampong-gelam/hajjah-fatimah-mosque/)

[![Image 21: Explore little India & its ancient culture & heritage](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/little-india-d.jpg) #### Little India Step into the vibrant tapestry of Singapore's Little India, an Indian cultural haven brimming with heritage, excitement, and endless charm.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/little-india/)

[![Image 22: Singapore Sidecar Tour on vintage Vespa sidecar](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/singapore-sidecar-tour-d.jpg) #### Singapore Sidecar Tour Uncover Singapore's heritage in a vintage Vespa sidecar and feel the wind in your face while exploring cultural landmarks.](https://www.sideways.sg/)

[![Image 23: Explore rich history at Kampong Gelam area](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/kampong-gelam-d.jpg) #### Kampong Gelam Explore rich history, living traditions, excellent eateries and trendy shops in this vibrant neighborhood.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/kampong-gelam/)

[![Image 24: Refurbished creative cluster new Bahru with heritage, retail and lifestyle experiences.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/746x746-things_to_do-cultural-new_bahru.jpg) #### New Bahru Built on the former Nan Chiau High School, New Bahru at River Valley celebrates heritage through retail, dining, and lifestyle experiences by over 40 homegrown brands.](https://www.newbahru.com/)

[![Image 25: Guided tour of 'The Istana' presidential residence](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/the-istana-d.jpg) #### The Istana Get a glimpse of the official residence of the President of the Republic of Singapore through guided tours of its majestic architecture and history.](https://www.istana.gov.sg/)
