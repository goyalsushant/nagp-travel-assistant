# Top Things To Do - Museums & Galleries

Museums and galleries for curious minds and culture lovers. These spaces are filled with stories, history, and discovery for visitors of all ages.

For upcoming museum tours, talks and events, head over to [Catch.sg](https://www.catch.sg/Event?Genre=Art+Galleries%2CMuseums&utm_source=visitsingapore&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vs_museum_artgalleries&utm_term=), Singapore's national platform on all things arts and culture.

[![Image 17: ArtScience Museum with Marina Bay Sands in the foreground](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/artscience-museum-d.jpg) #### ArtScience Museum Discover where art and science intersect with a wide range of exhibitions and events within the lotus-inspired building.](https://www.marinabaysands.com/museum.html)

[![Image 18: Interactive experience for childrens at 'Children's Museum'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/childrens-museum-d.jpg) #### Children's Museum Ignite your child’s curiosity at the Children's museum which offers immersive exhibits and interactive experiences for young minds.](https://www.heritage.sg/childrensmuseum)

[![Image 19: Mandai Gallery](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/neighbourhoods/mandai/746x620-mandai-mandai_gallery.jpg) #### Mandai Gallery Step into Mandai Gallery, an exhibition that celebrates the history and transformation of the Mandai Wildlife Reserve. Learn about how the reserve has contributed to nation-building, its commitment to sustainability and its efforts in wildlife conservation both locally and regionally.](https://www.mandai.com/en/mandai-gallery.html?cmp=sgcuriosity_cove%7Cref%7Cvs%7Cpar%7Cstb%7Cthings_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_museum_galleries)

[![Image 20: Uncover nation's history at National Museum of Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/national-museum-of-singapore-d.jpg) #### National Museum of Singapore Uncover the nation's history, from colonial times to modern Singapore through immersive exhibits.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/national-museum-of-singapore/)

[![Image 21: Explore Asia's diverse culture at Asian Civilisation Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/asian-civilisations-museum-d.jpg) #### Asian Civilisations Museum Journey through Asia's diverse cultures, exploring art, artifacts and captivating exhibitions.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/asians-civilisations-museum/)

[![Image 22: Hell’s Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/top_things_to_do-museum-hells_museum.jpg) #### Hell’s Museum Explore perspectives on life’s biggest mysteries across religions, cultures, and civilizations at this museum dedicated to death and the afterlife. Located in Haw Par Villa, it offers an immersive experience that includes the infamous 10 Courts of Hell.](https://www.hawparvilla.sg/hells-museum/)

[![Image 23: Vintage toys collection at Mint Museum of Toy](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/780x440-things_to_do-mint.jpg) #### MINT Museum of Toys Home to Asia’s largest collection of vintage toys and collectibles, this museum showcases childhood treasures from over 50 countries. Visitors can look forward to an interactive AI museum ambassador, and rediscover the wonder of play.](https://emint.com/)

[![Image 24: Contemporary Southeast Asian arts at 'Singapore Art Museum'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/singapore-art-museum-d.jpg) #### Singapore Art Museum Encounter contemporary Southeast Asian art, expressing cultural nuances and artistic brilliance.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/singapore-art-museum/)

[![Image 25: Changi Chapel Museum Landscape View](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/changi-chapel-museum-746x616.jpg) #### Changi Chapel and Museum Commemorate wartime history and take in poignant stories of bravery and resilience at the Changi Chapel and Museum.](https://www.heritage.sg/changichapelmuseum)

[![Image 26: Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/lee-kong-chian-natural-history-museum-d.jpg) #### Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum Marvel at nature's wonders, encounter dinosaurs, fossils and other diverse specimens. Immerse yourself in a world of biodiversity and conservation.](https://lkcnhm.nus.edu.sg/)

[![Image 27: Explore modern Southeast Asian art at National Gallery Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/national-gallery-singapore-d.jpg) #### National Gallery Singapore Discover the world's largest public display of modern Southeast Asian art within a restored City Hall and former Supreme Court building.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/national-gallery-singapore/)

[![Image 28: Singapore Maritime Gallery](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/museum-and-galleries/singapore-maritime-gallery-desktop.jpg) #### Singapore Maritime Gallery Uncover Singapore’s rich maritime heritage and how it is a leading maritime centre, through simulations, tours, exhibitions and more.](https://www.mpa.gov.sg/events-careers/public-outreach/singapore-maritime-gallery)
