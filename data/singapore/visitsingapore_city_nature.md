# Top Things To Do - CIty in Nature

Singapore is a city with green escapes, scenic walks, and photo-worthy nature spots. Explore our network of ecological corridors that connect nature reserves and parks, and experience nature in our urban environment.

### Gardens by the Bay

Marvel at the fusion of urban architecture and lush greenery in this breathtaking garden.

[![Image 18: Jewel Changi Airport Waterfall Experience](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/jewel-changi-airport-d_1561%20%C3%97%20878.jpg)](https://www.jewelchangiairport.com/en.html)

### Jewel Changi Airport

Experience nature's splendor with a stunning indoor waterfall amidst world-class shopping and dining at Jewel Changi.

[Find out More](https://www.jewelchangiairport.com/en.html)

[![Image 19: Family looking at a sloth in Mandai Wildlife reserve](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/1560x878-things_to_do-mandai.jpg)](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/mandai/)

### Mandai Wildlife Reserve

Observe the beauty of nature in action at Mandai Wildlife Reserve and learn how we are creating a better future for our wildlife and planet.

[Find Out More](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/mandai/)

[![Image 20: Fort Canning Centre Sign Name](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/fort-canning-park-d.jpg) #### Fort Canning Park Uncover ancient relics and historical gardens amidst the urban bustle or catch the occasional concert or theatre production happening.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/fort-canning-park)

[![Image 21: Relaxing beaches at Lazarus island](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/lazarus-island-d.jpg) #### Lazarus Island Bask in the sun, sand, and sea at Lazarus Island. This hidden gem boasts relaxing beaches and abundant marine life, just a ferry ride away from the mainland.](https://www.sla.gov.sg/articles/lazarus-island/)

[![Image 22: The pristine sandy beaches and lush greenery of St. John's Island which offers a peaceful escape for nature lovers](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/st-johns-Island-746x498.jpg) #### St John's Island Bask in the sun, sand, and sea at St. John's Island. This hidden gem boasts relaxing beaches and abundant marine life, just a ferry ride away from the mainland.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/sisters-islands-marine-park/activities/hiking-nature-walks-tours)

[![Image 23: Intertidal Walking Trails at Changi Beach](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/intertidal-trails-at-chani-m_786%C3%97860.jpg) #### Intertidal Trails at Changi Uncover marine wonders and coastal serenity through the intertidal trails at Changi.](https://theuntamedpaths.com/)

[![Image 24: Panorama View of Couples at Pulau Ubin](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/pulau-ubin-d.jpg) #### Pulau Ubin Step back in time on this island with cycling trails, kayaking and a rustic charm like no other.](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/)

[![Image 25: Drone view of Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/sungei-buloh-wetland-reserve-d.jpg) #### Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve Discover wildlife, lush mangroves and serene landscapes at this captivating wetland sanctuary.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/sungei-buloh-wetland-reserve)

[![Image 26: Bird Fly Serene Kranji Marshes](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/kranji-marshes-d.jpg) #### Kranji Marshes Escape to the serene beauty of Kranji Marshes, immerse yourself in lush wetlands, diverse wildlife, and tranquil trails.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/kranji-marshes)

[![Image 27: Singapore Botanic Gardens Pond](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/singapore-botanical-gardens-d.jpg) #### Singapore Botanic Gardens A harmonious blend of flora and fauna, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is vibrant with history.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/SBG)

[![Image 28: Drone View of The Southern Ridges Walking Trails](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/city-in-nature/the-southern-ridges-d.jpg) #### The Southern Ridges Embark on a scenic hike through lush treetop walks and iconic city vistas. Catch the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore and its iconic wave-like structure.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/when-visiting-parks/about-parks-nature-reserves-pcns/nature-park-networks-areas)
