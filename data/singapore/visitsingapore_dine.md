# Top Things To Do - Dine

Taste the cultures and cuisines that shape Singapore’s food scene. Try local comfort dishes, innovative dining concepts, and celebrated restaurants.

[![Image 17: Get local style Singaporean ramen at 'A Noodle Story'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/a-noodle-story.jpg) #### A Noodle Story Taste the fusion of flavours in handmade noodles, blending local and international influences.](https://www.facebook.com/ANoodleStory/)

[![Image 18: Natural light indoor seating at Caffe Fernet](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/746x746-things_to_do-eat-cafe_fernet.jpg) #### Caffe Fernet A modern Italian restaurant and bar on the waterfront, serving moreish plates and handmade pastas, with stunning views of Marina Bay.](https://www.caffefernet.sg/)

[![Image 19: Labyrinth, a Michelin starred restaurant](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/labyrinth.jpg) #### Labyrinth Take your tastebuds on an adventure at this Michelin-starred restaurant,which uses local flavours and homegrown produce.](https://www.restaurantlabyrinth.com/)

[![Image 20: Dining experience in the dark at 'NOX'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/nox.jpg) #### NOX Dine in the Dark Experience 12 mystery dishes served by visually impaired waiters in complete darkness. Be ready for a multi-sensory dining experience that will tantalise your taste buds and open your mind.](https://www.noxdineinthedark.com/)

[![Image 21: Andsoforth immersive dining experience](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/andsoforth.jpg) #### Andsoforth Experience immersive dining adventures with ever-changing themes and theatrical elements.](https://www.andsoforth.com.sg/)

[![Image 22: Singapore's locally-inspired cocktails on tap](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/746x746-things_to_do-dine-draftland.jpg) #### Draft Land Sip on a locally-inspired drink at this first-ever bar in Singapore, serving cocktails on tap.](https://linktr.ee/draftlandsg)

[![Image 23: Lau Pa Sat, a historic hawker centre](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/lau-pa-sat.jpg) #### Lau Pa Sat This historic hawker centre will take you on a gastronomic adventure of diverse flavours and vibrant atmosphere.](https://www.laupasat.sg/)

[![Image 24: Local spread of heritage cuisine in singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/746x746-things_to_do-eat-rempapa.jpg) #### Rempapa Enjoy Singapore’s diverse cuisine with Chef Damian, whose culinary creations resonate with diners across generations.](https://gilmore.sg/rempapa)

[![Image 25: Cable car Sky Dining with breathtaking view](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/cable-car-sky-dining.jpg) #### Cable Car Dining Dine amidst breathtaking views aboard a cable car, soaring over Sentosa Island and the cityscape.](https://mountfaberdining.com/pages/cable-car-sky-dining)

[![Image 26: 'Chilli Crab - A seafood dish'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/home-of-seafood-746x746.jpg) #### Home of Seafood A seafood paradise with tantalising dishes and fresh catches, Home of Seafood will delight all seafood lovers.](https://homeofseafood.com/index.html)

[![Image 27: Explore authentic Singaporean cuisine at Mutiara Seafood](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/tiles/mutiara-seafood.jpg) #### Mutiara Seafood Indulge in the freshest seafood at Mutiara Seafood, where authentic Singaporean cuisine and Halal-certified dishes celebrate tradition.](https://www.mutiaraseafood.com/)

[![Image 28: Royal Albatross Luxury tall ship with dinner cruise](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/dine/royal-albatross-sunset-sail-and-dinner-cruise-746%20%C3%97%20498.jpg) #### Royal Albatross Dine and sail on an evening cruise with Royal Albatross, or lunch at the Albatross Island Restaurant in the Southern Islands, reachable by Albatross speedboats from Marina South Pier, Resorts World Sentosa, or Ola Beach Club.](https://www.tallship.com.sg/events/sunset-sail-city-lights/)
