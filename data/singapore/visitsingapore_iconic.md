# Top Things To Do - Iconic Architecture

Colonial buildings, bold designs, and world-famous city landmarks. Discover the architectural attractions that define Singapore’s skyline.

[![Image 17: Landscape view of Chijmes, Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/chijmes-d.jpg) #### CHIJMES Enter into an enchanting world, where history meets elegance. Marvel at the stunning architecture of this restored convent turned dining and entertainment complex.](https://chijmes.com.sg/)

[![Image 18: Kranji War Memorial Landscape View](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/kranji-war-memorial-d.jpg) #### Kranji War Memorial Pay respects to the fallen soldiers at Kranji War Memorial, and find solace in this solemn and poignant site.](https://www.cwgc.org/visit-us/find-cemeteries-memorials/cemetery-details/2004200/kranji-war-cemetery/)

[![Image 19: Iconic Raffles hotel in Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/raffles-hotel-d.jpg) #### Raffles Hotel Step back in time to experience colonial architecture where luxury and heritage seamlessly blend together at the Raffles Hotel.](https://www.raffles.com/singapore/)

[![Image 20: Discover the Esplanade in Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/esplanade-d.jpg) #### Esplanade Discover captivating performances and embrace the unique, durian-inspired architecture that sets the Esplanade apart.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/esplanades-theatres-on-the-bay/)

[![Image 21: Marina Bay Sands landscape view](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/whats-happening/events/756x560_thingstodo_card_img_marinabaysands.png) #### Marina Bay Sands Experience luxury and breathtaking views at Marina Bay Sands. Indulge in world-class entertainment, dining, and the iconic rooftop infinity pool.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/marina-bay-sands/)

[![Image 22: Asia's largest observation wheel 'Singapore Flyer'](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/singapore-flyer-d.jpg) #### Singapore Flyer Soar above the city on Asia's largest observation wheel and marvel at panoramic views of Marina Bay.](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/singapore-flyer/)

[![Image 23: Henderson Waves Bridge, the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/henderson-waves-bridge-d.jpg) #### Henderson Waves Bridge Experience the stunning wave-like design at the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore. Be awed by panoramic views and observe a rich variety of flora and fauna.](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/telok-blangah-hill-park/special-features/henderson-waves)

[![Image 24: Old Hill Street Police Station](https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/dam/visitsingapore/things-to-do/ionic-architecture/old-hill-street-police-station-d.jpg) #### Old Hill Street Police Station Admire the vibrant rainbow hues which add a touch of charm to this historic building, now home to various government agencies.](https://www.mddi.gov.sg/who-we-are/ohsps/)
